## Response exclusions apply wherever an answer is repeated

The exclusions cover response_text, parsed_response, candidate_controls, populated prompt/input portions repeating model outputs, copied actual_controls, individual validator verdicts, and response copies in solver_scores or solver_errors. A renamed field, parsed JSON representation, scientific report or sanitized projection does not turn a model output into human-authored content.

For observation projections, request model/settings/schema and author-written prompt portions are separable research material; candidate-answer portions remain generated evidence. Usage, model/status identifiers, SDK version, latency, retry metadata and unknown attempt/cost fields are telemetry or factual records, not an automatic CC BY grant over provider material.

Oracle expected_controls and deterministic selection outputs are not Gemini answers merely because their control names overlap. Computed counts, correctness and costs are factual calculations, not new provider-generated prose.

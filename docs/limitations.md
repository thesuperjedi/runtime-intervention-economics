# Limitations and interpretation

- Synthetic, deliberately selected policy cases do not estimate production prevalence. Renderings/repetitions share facts; treat canonical cases as dependence units, and note that cases share a policy/generator too.
- E1 and EX1 are separate populations and execution regimes. Do not pool percentages or interpret differences as causal difficulty effects.
- Strong correctness270/270 E1 and180/180 EX1 is finite evidence, not zero future error. Conditional overlap when strong fails remains empirically unidentified.
- Cheap validators falsely accept some weak errors; a shared causal failure mechanism is plausible but not isolated.
- Two missing EX1 strong-validator judgments remain missing. Primary strong/common comparisons exclude two complete cases:58/174. Cheap supplementary comparison retains60/180. Valid solver pairs remain in Stage1.
- Human D/C/F competence, equal monetary loss per wrong set, review prices and hypothetical strong errors are assumptions. Grid-cell winners are not empirical probabilities.
- Cascades reuse frozen outputs; no live conditional cascade was executed. Strong validator and strong solver are different calls.
- Normalized token-derived USD is a list-price estimate; provider billing scope, account prices, currency conversion and failed-call charges are not resolved by this package.
- E0/E1 retry metadata did not establish zero transport retries. Later effective zero policy is configuration evidence, not measured HTTP attempt count.
- Original/public hashes provide traceability, not third-party authentication or pre-baseline historical byte guarantees.
- E0 execution source, paid runners, operational recovery and superseded prototypes/results are deliberately absent. Do not use the old zero-cost economics or three-class human prototype.
- Governance can prohibit or require an action regardless of its scalar expected monetary cost. No operational or governance validation follows.
- Security scans and tests are bounded checks. No legal redistribution approval, complete security clearance or novelty claim is given.

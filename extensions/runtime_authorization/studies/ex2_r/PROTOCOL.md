# EX2-R methods projection

A separate exploratory conditional-recovery experiment: two conditions, two models and three repetitions, twelve prespecified episodes. The exact order is retained in evidence.json.

Actual frozen PAP evaluates harness-generated P001 as R5 VENDOR_BLOCKED or CC_NOT_DELEGATED. Each independently verified seed issues no token, mutates no state and executes no effect. It is not model failure or incremental generation cost.

Models see labelled denial context without a worked solution. Recovery permits at most two model generations/proposals, M01/M02 and P002/P003. A third proposal cannot execute. Compliant completion requires authenticated final purchase effects and correct PR/ledger, not submission alone.

All twelve episodes remain in the primary denominator. Invalid seeds, accounted model failures and infrastructure uncertainty are distinct. Terminal HOLD ends the study without retry, resumption or replacement; liabilities remain held. Financial/execution authority is separate from protocol adoption.

Secondary measures cover calls, policy evaluations, denials, invalid outputs, effects, tokens, reservations and conditional costs. Seed evaluations are separate from model evaluations. Twelve seeds, zero compliant recoveries and zero unauthorized effects were observed. Six Gemini episodes ended in denial and six Claude episodes in explicit terminals. Deterministic feasible paths were established separately offline, not inferred from model success. A separate source-mapped local deterministic recovery demonstration is included under a proposed MIT scope; it reuses original PAP/driver/oracle logic and omits empirical provider/accounting/journal infrastructure. It does not alter these model outcomes.

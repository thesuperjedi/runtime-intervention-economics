# Experimental evidence, provenance and redistribution limitations

This candidate preserves540 E1,1620 E2 and720 EX1 sanitized observation projections. Two EX1 terminal HTTP503 failures remain failed; missing judgments, usage and charges remain unknown. No response was fabricated, replaced or zero-filled.

## Distinct origins and rights

1. **Original research material:** author-controlled software, tests, documentation, synthetic policy expression, case selection/rendering methods and eligible compilation material. MIT or CC BY4.0 applies as scoped in LICENSE_SCOPE.md.
2. **Gemini-generated experimental responses:**900 control-set answers and1978 validator judgments across E1/E2/EX1, plus four E0 marker responses in the compact summary. These are provider-generated observations, not exclusively human-authored material. No automatic author CC BY grant is applied.
3. **Provider telemetry and factual measurements:** normalized usage counts, model/status names, SDK version, latency, retry policy, unknown HTTP-attempt counts and null actual-charge fields. Their inclusion does not transfer provider rights or imply copyright in facts.
4. **Derived science:** exact-set scores, confusion counts, reconstructed routes, cost estimates and sensitivity equations. These calculations are not fresh model outputs. Any exact response copies embedded in reports retain the generated-evidence exclusion.

Public response representations include response_text/parsed_response and duplicated candidate answers in request prompts/inputs, candidate_controls, actual_controls, solver_scores or solver_errors. The exclusion follows the content rather than its directory or field name.

## What provenance establishes

Original/public mapping and separate SHA256 inventories link private frozen bytes to sanitized public bytes. Provider model identity, role, settings, joins, normalized usage and retry metadata are retained. SDK-serialized dumps, interaction IDs, opaque signatures, host metadata, execution timestamps and raw exception bodies are excluded.

Successful projection reproduction establishes the scientific calculations from these public records. It does not independently authenticate unavailable historical raw records, prove a single HTTP attempt, validate actual invoice charges or make fresh model execution reproducible. Paid executors and credentials are absent.
